RAG -> Information retrival + text generation
4 steps:-
1. Indexing
2. Retrieval
3. Augmentaion
4. Generation

Indexing: 
1 Document Ingestion = You load your source knowledge into memory
2 Text Chunking = Break large documents into small , semantically meaningful chunks
3 Embedding = Convert each chunk to dense vector that capture its meaning
4 storage in vector store = store the vectors along with the original chunk text + metadata in vector database

Retrieval:
Retrieval is the real-time process of finding the most relevant pieces of information
from a pre-built index (created during indexing) based on the user's question 

Augmentaion:
Augmentaion refers to the step where the retrieved documents(chunk of relevant context) are 
combined with the user's query to form a new , enriched prompt for the LLM

Generation:
Generation  is the final step where a Large Language Model (llm) uses the 
user's query and the retrieved and augmented context to generate a response
